03 Why Logos

On LEZ, privacy is an account type.

LEZ PRIVATE ACCOUNTS
Your private account runs the public lending program inside its own proof. One transaction: private on your side, public on the market's.
LEZ SEQUENCER
Verifies the proof with LEZ's privacy circuit. We didn't write a shielded pool or a verifier.
LEZ WALLET
Keys, mnemonic and private state stay on this laptop. Positions can be recovered from the mnemonic.
LOGOS BASECAMP
The app is a Basecamp module, wallet and markets included, over a C ABI to our client.

Without Logos: build your own shielded pool, verifier and wallet first. Here they're the platform.

06 Impact · Privacy Pools on LEZ

Dirty money? Prove it isn't.

  1. A public deposit gets a label. Where the money came from is on record.
  2. A screener approves clean labels into a Merkle root. Sanctioned sources stay out.
  3. A withdrawal proves its label is under the root. Not which label it is.
  4. Not approved? Take it back. Only the depositor can, and they always can.

A1 Appendix · pseudonymous positions

Positions stay public. Owners don't.

STAYS IN THE PROOF
secret spending keyfrom the wallet mnemonic
salt = HMAC(key, market ‖ owner ‖ index)
seed = SHA256(market ‖ owner ‖ salt)
position = PDA(lending, seed)collateral, debt, health: publicPUBLISHED

Anyone can check health and liquidate. Lenders never depend on a borrower being online.

Two positions of one owner get unrelated addresses.

Lose the laptop, keep the mnemonic: positions are found again by walking index 0, 1, 2…

Lenders can go further: shares held on their own private account. No public position at all.

A2 Appendix · why lenders stay safe

The programs are blind. We made that safe.

On LEZ a program can't read state while it plans a transaction, and each step sees one account.

So every operation carries claims about what it can't see: a ratio interval, a price interval, a window in time. Each account checks the claims that concern it.

The maths in core makes sure any value inside a claim leaves lenders no worse off. Rounding always goes their way.

A4 Appendix · the app

Health is a water level.

Debt over borrowing power, drawn so that health 1.0 sits on the sell line. Ask for too much and the app says so before anything is sent.

Calm water, Safe: health ≥ 1.5 Rising: 1.2 to 1.5 Nearly over: under 1.2

You see all of it. Nobody else sees whose it is.
